About Us · 关于我们

Joyful Notes Children's Choir

Joyful Notes Children’s Choir was founded in June 2023 by conductor Shana Yang in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. This government registered non-profit organization is committed to providing children and youth with rich musical education and performance opportunities, creating a sense of belonging, nurturing personal growth, and ensuring singers experience the benefits of community.

The choir’s mandate is to promote multiculturalism in Canada.
